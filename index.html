GOOGLE DRIVE CONNECTION

Why Google access is requested

The owner authorizes Google Drive access so the tool can verify the account and folder privacy, check available space, upload encrypted backups, download them for recovery checks, and move eligible older backups to Trash.

The Google permission grants broad Drive access. The tool's own rules limit file operations to its marked backup archives in the designated folder. Google does not enforce that folder limit through this permission.

This page provides information only. Google authorization is started privately by the owner. There is no public registration or Google sign-in on this website.

TERMS OF USE

For G-Fit Tech's internal test work

G-Fit Tech operates this tool for its owner's authorized test database and Google account. It is not offered as a public backup service. Only the owner may connect accounts or operate the automation.

Recovery checks cover the data and database features included in each backup. They do not guarantee uninterrupted service or complete recovery of every external service. The owner is responsible for keeping a separate recovery key and reviewing backup results.

The tool and these terms may change as testing develops. Questions can be sent to gfittechnology@gmail.com.
